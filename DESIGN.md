## Brand & Style
This design system marries the calculated precision of high-end motorsport telemetry with the craft of haute couture technical apparel printing. It is engineered for discerning motorsport enthusiasts, bespoke apparel clients, and high-performance teams who demand absolute precision, stealth luxury, and technical credibility.

The aesthetic philosophy is **Stealth High-Performance Minimalist**:
- **Monolithic Darkness:** Layers of deep obsidian and forged carbon dark tones (`#070709`, `#121217`) form a void-like canvas that eliminates visual noise and heightens focus on apparel silhouettes and garment textures.
- **Calibrated Chromatics:** Blinding strikes of pure racing red serve as functional telemetry spikes, counterbalanced by microscopic cyan and magenta registration accents that pay homage to sub-millimeter print registration.
- **Architectural Edge:** Strict geometric grids, ultra-refined titanium hairline boundaries, and restrained structural typography evoke aerospace dashboard telemetry and racecraft cockpits.

## Colors
The palette is rooted in dark-mode engineering, where chromatic saturation is deployed with razor discipline:

- **Primary (`#E4161C` / `#EF4444`):** Pure telemetry red. Strictly reserved for high-impact actions, critical telemetry alerts, live lap triggers, and direct brand signifiers.
- **Secondary (`#71717A` / Titanium Muted):** Industrial titanium tones for structural balance, secondary navigational elements, and descriptive metadata.
- **Tertiary (`#06B6D4` / `#00F0FF`):** Cyan print precision mark. Applied sparingly as sub-millimeter data points, active calibration ticks, and technical proof tags.
- **Neutral (`#070709` / `#121217` / `#1A1A22`):** Carbon-graded dark surfaces. Void blacks create deep recessive fields, while elevated carbon levels build structural tiers without relying on heavy borders or garish tints.
- **Podium Gold (`#D4AF37`):** Reserved strictly for luxury tier badges, limited-edition garment drops, and VIP paddock verification tokens.

## Typography
The typographical hierarchy balances raw technical acceleration with pristine editorial poise:

- **Display & Headings (Space Grotesk):** Engineered with crisp, geometric proportions and mechanical quirks that echo instrument dials and digital race consoles. All display treatments use tightened tracking to project density and power.
- **Body & Data Content (Manrope):** Geometric yet open and humanized, delivering pristine legibility in multi-line product specifications, sizing manuals, and luxury fabric descriptors across dark backgrounds.
- **Labels & Telemetry Micro-copy (Space Grotesk):** Rendered in uppercase with generous tracking (`0.08em` to `0.15em`). Used for serial codes, apparel print coordinates, edition numbers, and interactive status badges.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout reflects a rigid modular chassis:

- **Grid Structure:** A 12-column responsive fluid grid pinned within an ultra-wide `1440px` safe boundary. Desktop uses `1.5rem` gutters with `3rem` section margins. Mobile reflows dynamically into 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` outer canvas padding.
- **Density Control:** Technical dashboards and customization consoles deploy dense vertical rhythms (`space-xs` to `space-md`) to emulate telemetry monitors. Showroom and lookbook narratives expand into panoramic intervals using `space-lg` and `space-xl` to establish quiet, high-end prestige.
- **Reflow & Docking:** On mobile, split navigation strips collapse into an anchored bottom cockpit dock. Telemetry tables convert into horizontally swipeable, snap-aligned module cards.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is articulated through titanium edge luminescence, subtle carbon tiering, and laser-guided light emissions rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - *Base Void:* `#070709` provides pure zero-ground depth.
  - *Chassis Surfaces:* `#121217` defines executive panels, preview docks, and primary containers.
  - *Elevated Flight Decks:* `#1A1A22` supports floating drawers, active context modals, and popovers.
- **Hairline Precision Borders:** Surfaces rely on `1px` borders colored in `#272730` (Titanium Border) or `#3F3F4E` under interaction. Heavy drop shadows are banned.
- **Laser Ambient Glows:** High-priority items (active race telemetry indicators, cart triggers, checkout states) emit a razor-focused ambient halo: `0px 0px 24px -4px rgba(228, 22, 28, 0.35)`.
- **Glass Shrouds:** Overlays utilize backdrop filtration (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) blended over `#070709` at 85% opacity, simulating tinted motorsport helmet visors.

## Shapes
A disciplined **Soft (`1`)** roundedness governs the entire design system:

- Base elements (buttons, inputs, micro-tags) feature a strict `0.25rem` (4px) curvature, reminiscent of carbon-fiber cutouts and CNC-milled chassis components.
- Medium modules and dashboard cards take `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px).
- Modal assemblies, display frames, and technical hero viewports cap at `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem` / 12px).
- Continuous circular pills (`rounded-full`) are reserved exclusively for status indicators, active radio pills, and print registration crosshairs to contrast against the architectural geometry.

## Components

### Buttons & Trigger Elements
- **Primary Telemetry Button:** Grounded in `#E4161C`, typography in crisp `#FFFFFF` `Space Grotesk` (`label-lg`), with a `0.25rem` radius. On hover, background shifts to `#EF4444` backed by a `0px 0px 20px rgba(228, 22, 28, 0.4)` glow.
- **Secondary Titanium Button:** Transparent core framed by a `1px` `#272730` border, resting on `#121217`. Transitions to `#1A1A22` background with `#71717A` border on hover.
- **Ghost Action:** Borderless, neutral-muted text that illuminates to pure white with a `2px` racing red underline highlight on hover.

### Interactive Status Pills & Badges
- Compact capsules (`rounded-full`) featuring a `2px` leading optical beacon dot.
- *Active/Live:* Cyan (`#00F0FF`) beacon with a faint cyan border and translucent `#06B6D415` fill.
- *Paddock VIP / Limited:* Gold beacon (`#D4AF37`) with micro-caps uppercase typography.
- *Telemetry Warning / Critical:* Pulsing `#E4161C` indicator dot over an obsidian backdrop.

### Executive Dashboard Cards & Panels
- Constructed on `#121217` with a razor `1px` `#272730` perimeter.
- Headers integrate uppercase Space Grotesk section dividers alongside simulated CMYK registration marks (quadrant cyan/magenta hairline crosses) in the top-right corner.
- Internal metrics utilize high-contrast white digits paired with muted titanium telemetry labels.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background set to `#070709` inset within `#121217` surfaces.
- Bordered in `1px` `#272730` with `0.25rem` curvature.
- Focus state triggers an immediate crisp `#E4161C` border with zero blur, accompanied by an uppercase floating telemetry tag.

### Custom Apparel Configurator Components
- **Fabric/Thread Swatch Selector:** Precision circular swatches framed in titanium bezels; selected state displays a double concentric ring with a cyan `#00F0FF` inner border.
- **Print Spec Readout:** Technical terminal list styled in `Space Grotesk` `body-sm` with alternating hairline dividers (`#1A1A22`), matching garment mockups to real-time vector coordinates.
